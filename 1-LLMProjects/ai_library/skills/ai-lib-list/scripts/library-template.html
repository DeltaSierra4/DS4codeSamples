This library records what a collection of documents says. A document being here implies nothing about whether it is correct. A first-party announcement is a marketing artifact as well as a technical one; a preprint has not been reviewed; a blog post may have been edited since it was captured. Every claim shows the marker naming where it came from, and the four kinds are not interchangeable — a page locator, an unlocated claim, a linked-page claim and a reader's inference are four different things.
